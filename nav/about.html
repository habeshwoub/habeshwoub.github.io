About

Research grounded in evidence and practical impact.

I investigate how software teams can build, evolve, and govern dependable systems when requirements, technologies, and organizational environments continuously change.

Biography

I am Woubshet Behutiye, PhD, a software engineering researcher and educator based in Oulu, Finland. My research spans requirements engineering, software quality, agile software development, technical debt, evidence-based software engineering, digital health, and software platforms and ecosystems.

My current work examines how foundation models, large language models, and agentic AI change software-engineering practices, education, platform architecture, and ecosystem governance. I am particularly interested in human-centred approaches that combine technological capability with quality, accountability, and responsible adoption.

I collaborate with academic, industrial, and healthcare partners and translate research findings into methods, tools, prototypes, and teaching interventions.
