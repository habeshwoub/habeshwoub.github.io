Teaching and supervision

Learning through authentic software-engineering practice.

I use project-based, research-informed, and industry-connected teaching to help students reason about complex requirements, systems, and ecosystems.

Current courses

Course leadership and teaching

My teaching connects foundational software-engineering concepts with contemporary practice, including foundation models and agentic systems. I teach these courses at the master's and bachelor's levels at the University of Oulu, in Oulu, Finlandand Nanjing Institute of Technology, Nanjing, China.

Master's level

Software Platforms and Ecosystems

Platform strategy, architecture, APIs, network effects, governance, deployment, data spaces, and AI-native and agentic ecosystems.

Bachelor's level

Requirements Engineering

Elicitation, specification, prioritization, modelling, validation, requirements management, and responsible use of generative AI.

Master's level

Professional Software Engineering Processes, and Human Factors

Agile practices, professional teamwork, software quality, testing, continuous engineering, and human aspects of development.

Teaching approach

How students learn

01

Authentic cases

Students work with realistic platform, healthcare, and software-product scenarios.

02

Active learning

Short exercises, discussions, role play, mapping, peer critique, and iterative project work.

03

Critical AI literacy

Students learn to use AI tools while verifying outputs and retaining professional judgement.

04

Evidence and reflection

Research findings inform the course, and student reflection makes learning visible.

Supervision

I supervise bachelor's and master's theses and contribute to doctoral research supervision. Suitable topics include requirements engineering, software quality, technical debt, AI in software engineering, digital health, and software platforms and ecosystems.
