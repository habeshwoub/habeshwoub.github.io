Research programme

From sound requirements to sustainable ecosystems

I study how software-intensive systems can remain useful, trustworthy, and adaptable as technologies, organizations, and stakeholder needs evolve.

RE

Requirements and quality

Methods, evidence, and tools for eliciting, documenting, validating, and managing quality requirements in evolving systems.

AI

AI-enabled engineering

Human-centred uses of foundation models, multi-agent systems, PromptOps, and LLMOps in software engineering and education.

PE

Platforms and ecosystems

Architecture, governance, interoperability, and evolution across digital platforms, software ecosystems, and agentic ecosystems.
